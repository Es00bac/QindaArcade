# Art revision 2

Original art created with the built-in image-generation tool (generate mode),
then copied into this project. No external API/CLI or purchased assets.
All three output images are 1254 × 1254 (the requested size was 2048 × 2048).

- `textures/enamel-wear-v2.png`: neutral enamel detail; tinted per authored part,
  sampled with metric UVs and used for chip/roughness response.
- `textures/meadow-ground-v2.png`: calmer grass/earth ground albedo.
- `textures/timber-v2.png`: weathered timber; grain follows each plank's long axis.
- `textures/racer-liveries-v2.png` and `.svg`: editable native Cairo graphics,
  generated by `tools/export_liveries.cpp`, not image-generated text. Four padded
  islands per racer: number, sponsor, bumper sticker, transparent spray/stencil.
- `textures/kart-0-uv.svg` through `kart-7-uv.svg`: per-racer livery island guides.
  Curved decal meshes conform to the nose; flat ones follow the panel slope.
- `models/racers-and-world-v2.blend`: editable Blender MCP-authored source.
- `models/racers-and-world-v2.glb`: interchange source library.
- `models/art-v2-manifest.json`: bounds, material names and triangle counts.
- `../docs/RACERS.md`: character origins, sponsors, motivations and construction.

## Exact generation prompts

### Timber

```
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: seamless tileable game material albedo texture.
Primary request: a straight-on orthographic scan of weathered reclaimed timber, fine tightly spaced natural wood grain running vertically from top to bottom. Subtle small cracks and old sanding scratches, gray-brown sunbleached wood with a little remaining oil finish. Quiet irregular realistic grain, not exaggerated orange tiger stripes. This is a continuous wood surface, not an arrangement of boards: no plank seams, no nails, no big knots.
Composition: square full bleed tileable edges, 2048 by 2048, physical coverage about 1 meter.
Lighting: absolutely flat neutral albedo without baked shadows, highlights, perspective or vignette.
Constraints: muted desaturated taupe/brown palette, restrained contrast, no text, no watermark, no border.
```

### Enamel

```
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: seamless tileable game material base-color texture, a neutral wear source for eight individually painted racing karts and workshop props.
Primary request: an orthographic straight-on scan of a flat medium-light cool gray painted metal surface with restrained realistic age. Fine irregular scuffs, sparse hairline scratches and a few small localized flakes showing dull dark gray bare steel under gray enamel. 90 percent intact paint, subtle spray-can orange-peel grain. Small chips, not huge peeled patches. No rust clouds.
Composition: full-bleed square material texture, seamless edges, uniform scale, no framing or borders.
Lighting: neutral flat albedo, no baked directional lighting, no reflections, no shadow gradients.
Materials: satin enamel with believable tiny imperfections, close-up surface detail suitable for real-time physically based shading.
Constraints: monochrome grayscale ONLY, no color hue, no text, no logos, no objects, no panels or seams, no perspective, no watermark. Intended to be tinted separately by the engine; keep overall medium-light gray and low contrast, chips darker. 2048 by 2048.
```

### Meadow

```
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: seamless tileable game ground base-color texture for a sunny reclaimed junkyard meadow.
Primary request: orthographic top-down flat scan of closely cut wild meadow turf: fine muted olive and sage grass blades, tiny clover, straw flecks and irregular glimpses of brown compacted earth. Physically plausible ground cover at two meters square, not moss, not bright yellow-green, no flowers. Delicate variation, mostly grass with 15 percent exposed earth and dry stalks.
Composition: square full-bleed seamless tile, evenly distributed natural detail without obvious repeating focal objects.
Lighting: diffuse flat neutral albedo, no cast shadows, no directional lighting or vignette.
Constraints: restrained realistic saturation, no rocks or large leaves, no trash or props baked into the ground, no text, no watermark, no borders, no perspective. 2048 by 2048.
```
